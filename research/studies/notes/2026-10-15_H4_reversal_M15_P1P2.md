## Signal (M15 closed bars only)
Core source calculates raw %K(14), K=SMA(raw,3), D=SMA(K,5), corresponding to its actual code configuration `stoch_n=14, stoch_k=3, stoch_d=5`. **Do not silently swap smoothing 3 and 5**; prior discussion described these ambiguously as 14-5-3. Verify against MT5 input conventions before declaring parity.

BUY: K<20 -> K crosses above D -> K crosses above 20; SELL: K>80 -> K crosses below D -> K crosses below 80. One transition per closed bar. At a new H4 regime reset signal states and P counters. P1/P2 are the first/second completed same-direction signals within that regime. Count signal completions **before** downstream H4-level gating in `signal` mode; the core also supports `eligible` mode, which yields different P labels.
