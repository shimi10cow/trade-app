## Provisional H4 structural level algorithm (explicit proxy)
1. On H4 bars, a pivot high (low) must exceed (fall below) highs (lows) of two bars on each side.
2. Confirm pivot only after the two subsequent H4 bars have closed; do not use future prices in historical decisions.
3. If the previous closed H4 bar closes beyond the latest confirmed pivot, create a structural level at that pivot price. Activate at the current H4 bar opening.
4. Consume/expire the level on its first later H4 bar revisit: low <= level price <= high. Expiry effective at next H4 bar.
5. BUY rejection: an active level above entry and within 0.60 * completed H4 ATR14; SELL rejection: active level below entry within the same threshold.
6. Other signal and position logic is unchanged from the canonical engine.

**Limitation:** Original H4 structural level construction was not fully recovered. This proxy is not an exact reproduction of the historical 385-trade EURUSD baseline.
