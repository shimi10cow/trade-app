# Dow pivot / protected swing rules — 2026-10-18

Status: specification and snapshot of current implementation; NOT a completed/corrected EA.

## 1. Existing causal Dow pivot detector (unchanged)
Reference: dow_frozen_causal_v1.py, event-sourced adapter to research/dow_frozen_v1/dow_final_engine.py.
Params (ATR period, ATR multiplier, ER boost, ER cutoff):
W1 (7,1.9,+0.15,0.40)
D1 (7,1.9,-0.15,0.40)
H4 (7,2.0,+0.30,0.40)
H1 (7,2.4,-0.15,0.25)
M15 (7,2.1,+0.30,0.25).
TR=max(high-low, abs(high-prev_close), abs(low-prev_close)).
ATR=EWMA(TR,alpha=1/7,adjust=False).
ER20=abs(close[i]-close[i-20])/sum(abs(diff(close)),last 20).
threshold=ATR * multiplier * (1+boost if ER20>=cutoff else 1).
Detector alternates highs/lows. During rising leg, keep highest wick; confirm high when subsequent bar low falls >= threshold from that wick. During falling leg, keep lowest wick; confirm low when subsequent bar high rises >= threshold. Confirmed pivot retains anchor_time (where extreme happened) and known_at (when confirmation occurred). Latest pivot can be relocated when more extreme wick occurs; emit relocated event. Never use final historical pivot before known_at. Existing audit shows exact final pivot anchor parity for D1/H4/H1/M15; this does not prove downstream protected-swing correctness.

## 2. Dow break and protected swing (agreed 2026-10-18)
Break of a high: completed candle CLOSE > the high pivot's WICK; break of a low: CLOSE < low pivot's WICK. NOT full-body breakout. Single-candle reversal is allowed.
In uptrend, protected swing = formally known Dow low (押し安値); in downtrend = formally known Dow high (戻り高値).
When price closes above prior extreme high, adopt the corresponding newly recognized Dow low IF it exists and is structurally located between prior high and breakout. Otherwise keep old protected low unchanged. Do NOT invent an unconfirmed low, and do NOT enqueue one pending object per breakout bar.
If a Dow low becomes formally known later, inspect its ANCHOR TIME and already observed extreme-high breakout: if that low formed before the breakout and belongs to the corresponding leg, update protected low at its KNOWN_AT time. If low formed after the breakout, do not assign it retrospectively to that breakout; wait for its corresponding later breakout.
At every completed bar, after incorporating pivot confirmations/relocations that are known on that bar, check CLOSE against the currently valid protected level. CLOSE below protected low reverses up->down; CLOSE above protected high reverses down->up. Must not skip reversal even when candidate protected low/high has not been recognized or a move was missed. If new pivot confirmation and reversal coincide, apply causal event ordering deterministically; never backdate.
On reversal, record the previously protected swing as a horizontal zone. The newly initialized opposite protected point may use the prior trend segment's raw wick extreme (explicit exception), otherwise use recognized Dow pivots.
Keep one current protected line, not stale historical lines.

## 3. Provisional trend (仮トレンド)
Separate from major trend. Down: confirmed L1,H1,L2,H2, H2<H1; once L1 has been closed below, CLOSE below L2 starts provisional down, protected provisional high=H2, without waiting for L3. Mirror up. Continuation uses corresponding confirmed opposite pivot after new extreme break, with causal late confirmation. CLOSE beyond provisional protected level flips/ends provisional trend and generates corresponding zone. Avoid repeated breakout event floods.

## 4. Double top / bottom (W)
Major uptrend W-top: confirmed A high, B low, C high; between A and C no candle CLOSE above A wick (C wick may exceed A); CLOSE below B wick triggers B-based horizontal zone. Major downtrend W-bottom is symmetric. At most two W events/zones per major trend epoch; reset on major reversal. If B is also major protected swing, retain BOTH W and major labels/events while deduplicating the physical zone geometry. Same-bar W and major reversal both count.

## 5. Horizontal zone and chart
Outer price edge = pivot wick. Inner edge = nearest candle BODY edge to that wick in the relevant full structural wave, based only on bars known by activation. Start at pivot anchor, become visible only at activation/breakout. Expire at FIRST later candle whose entire BODY is beyond the opposite zone edge (support: max(open,close)<low; resistance: min(open,close)>high). Expiration cannot happen on activation candle. Preserve full event history and all historical zones for auditing; display may be restricted to zones generated since 2026-08, per timeframe, plus current protected swing.
Do NOT assign permanent support/resistance identity or color: same horizontal price zone can change role depending on current price. Label zones with source(s): 押し安値, 戻り高値, ダブルトップB, ダブルボトムB, 仮トレンド, etc. Use Complete chart as base, native editable chart objects, not a separate chart.
